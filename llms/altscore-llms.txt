# AltScore

> B2B credit infrastructure for Latin America — the "Rails of Lending" that lets any company build, embed, and deploy credit products in weeks. Three API modules: AltData (aggregation over 50+ data sources for underwriting/scoring), Borrower Central (onboarding, KYC/KYB, identities, documents, deals, workflows, decisioning), and a Credit Management System (loan lifecycle: credit accounts, debts, disbursements, payment orders, DPAs). Frontegg-backed OAuth/OIDC identity, first-party Python & TypeScript SDKs, a Go CLI, and a dedicated sandbox environment.

## APIs
- [AltData API](https://data.altscore.ai): Alternative-data aggregation for credit underwriting and scoring.
- [Borrower Central API](https://bc.altscore.ai): Onboarding, KYC/KYB, identities, documents, deals, workflows (v1/v2), decisioning.
- [Credit Management System API](https://api.altscore.ai): Partners, clients, credit accounts, debts, disbursements, payment orders, DPAs, webhooks.
